PIPELINE VALVE COMPONENT RULES AND STANDARDS
Technical Reference Document — Internal Use Only


This document describes the rules, constraints, and formatting conventions applied
to pipeline valve attributes within the corporate data management system.


VALVE TYPE

The valve type must be recorded using the approved short form from the internal
reference list IS-002. Full words are not accepted in data fields. The following
substitutions apply: GATE VALVE becomes GV, BALL VALVE becomes BV, BUTTERFLY VALVE
becomes BFV, and CHECK VALVE becomes CV. Any additional descriptive qualifiers
following the valve type, such as "full bore" or "wedge", must be preserved
unchanged after the abbreviation is applied. Reference: internal reference list
IS-002.


PRESSURE

All pressure values in the system must be expressed exclusively in megapascals (MPa).
The use of bar, atm, kgf/cm2, or psi is not permitted. If a source document contains
pressure in bar, convert it before entry: 1 bar equals 0.1 MPa. If the source uses
psi, apply the factor 1 psi = 0.006895 MPa. Nominal pressure must not exceed the
allowable rating for the given valve class. The standard nominal pressure series
includes PN 1.6, PN 2.5, and PN 4.0 MPa. Any value outside this series requires
explicit engineering justification. Reference: GOST 356-80, clause 3.1.


TEMPERATURE

Temperature values must be recorded in degrees Celsius only. Kelvin and Fahrenheit
are not accepted. To convert from Kelvin, subtract 273.15. To convert from
Fahrenheit, apply the formula (F minus 32) multiplied by 5/9. The working medium
temperature must not exceed the allowable limit for the selected body material.
For grey cast iron bodies, the maximum permissible temperature is plus 200 degrees
Celsius. For carbon steel bodies, this limit rises to plus 425 degrees Celsius.
Exceeding these limits without written approval from the engineering department
is not allowed. Reference: GOST 53402-2009, clause 4.2.


DIMENSIONS AND WEIGHT

All linear dimensions, including face-to-face length and nominal diameter, must
be expressed in millimetres. Inches are not accepted; apply 1 inch = 25.4 mm when
converting. The nominal diameter must belong to the standard DN series: 15, 20, 25,
32, 40, 50, 65, 80, 100, 125, 150, 200, and so on. Values outside this series are
not valid without special approval. The face-to-face length for a gate valve at
DN100 is 250 mm in the short dimensional series and 350 mm in the long series.
Weight must be given in kilograms. Pounds and grams are not accepted; apply
1 pound equals 0.4536 kilograms when converting. For DN100 PN16 cast iron gate
valves, the typical weight range is 16 to 22 kg depending on the specific design.
Reference: GOST 3326-86, GOST 28338-89.


MATERIALS

The body material must be compatible with both the working medium and the operating
temperature. Grey cast iron GG-20 is suitable for water and steam up to plus 200
degrees Celsius and for neutral liquids. Carbon steel grade 25L covers applications
up to plus 425 degrees Celsius. Sealing and contact surfaces must be manufactured
from corrosion-resistant material. The accepted standard is stainless steel grade
12Cr18Ni9Ti or a certified equivalent. Material codes must be recorded using the
approved short form from the internal reference list IS-002: Material codes are
GCI for grey cast iron, CS for carbon steel, and SS for stainless steel. Grade or
alloy designations following the material name, such as "GG-20" or "12Cr18Ni9Ti",
must be preserved unchanged after the abbreviation is applied. Reference:
GOST 32569-2013, table 1; GOST 5762-2002, clause 2.4.


LEAKAGE, CONNECTION AND FLANGE STANDARDS

The valve must comply with the leakage class declared in its specification. Class A
denotes zero leakage and is required for steam and gas applications. Class B is
acceptable for water service. The end connection type must correspond to the pressure
class and nominal diameter. Flanged connections are mandatory for DN 50 mm and above
at PN 1.0 MPa and higher. End connection types must be recorded using the approved
short form from the internal reference list IS-002: FLANGED becomes FLG, THREADED
becomes THD, WELDED becomes WLD, and SOCKET WELD becomes SW. Flange geometry must
comply with GOST 33259-2015, which supersedes the earlier GOST 12815-80. Standard
references, including flange standards, must follow the format GOST-33259 or
ASME-B16.5, without extra spaces or punctuation. Reference: GOST R 54808-2011,
table 2; GOST 33259-2015, clause 5.1.


ACTUATOR AND SERVICE LIFE

The actuator type is selected based on operating conditions and the required opening
torque. A manual handwheel is appropriate when the torque does not exceed 250 newton
metres. For higher torques, an electric or pneumatic actuator must be specified.
Actuator types must be recorded using the approved short form from the internal
reference list IS-002: MANUAL HANDWHEEL becomes MHW, ELECTRIC ACTUATOR becomes EA,
and PNEUMATIC ACTUATOR becomes PA. The minimum designated service life for pipeline
valves is 25 years, provided that installation, operation, and maintenance are
carried out in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions. Reference:
GOST 5762-2002, clause 3.7; GOST 53402-2009, clause 6.3.


CLIMATIC VERSION

The climatic version code must be written in uppercase with no spaces or separators
between the letters and the digit. The correct form is UHL1, not UHL 1 or uhl1.
Similarly, T3 and O1 are correct; T 3 and O 1 are not. UHL1 designates equipment
suitable for moderate and cold climates and for outdoor installation.
Reference: GOST 15150-69, table 1.
